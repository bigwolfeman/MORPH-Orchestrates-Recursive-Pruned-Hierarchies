# Agent Note: dmorph v1 — a no-loop MORPH trained with DiffusionBlocks-routed flow matching

Status: rejected — measured 2026-09-03 (lab/experiments/failures/2026-09-03-dmorph-v1-panel.md): the noisy stream costs the shipped head 0.15 (tok) / 0.40 (hs) nats at 0.69x throughput and its ladder never beats its one-pass read; dmorph is informational, the paid TUL loop stays the recipe (Wolfe, 2026-09-03)

## Problem

The paid TUL loop is the shipped forward (master `6962445`): it is the one arm whose
loop earns depth, and it costs 47 layer passes per token against 8.9 for the same
model with `n_core: 0`. At 20k steps on seq 1024 it sits 0.022 nats BEHIND the plain
model at 1.33x the wall clock, and in the shipped configuration the TUL phase runs at
7.2k tok/s against 49.6k tok/s in the TST phase
(`.agents/notes/implemented/architecture/2026-09-03-ship-the-paid-loop-cut-the-arms.md`).
Wolfe's question (2026-09-03): drop the loop, keep the Thought Unpack Loop's slot
geometry, and buy back what the loop bought with a DiffusionBlocks-structured
flow-matching objective — iterative refinement at inference that costs one forward of a
flat stack, and an explicit "predict the next thought" signal on the slot. Decide at
matched wall-clock AND matched tokens against the coreless bar, on token CE.

What the record already says, so v1 does not repeat it (every number is cited in
`lab/dmorph/research/`):

- DiffusionBlocks (arXiv 2506.14202) claims NO training-FLOP win; Appendix H proves
  compute equal to standard training. The claim is memory (`1/B`) and, for
  autoregressive text, parity on MAUVE and greedy teacher gen-PPL — never perplexity.
  The recurrent-depth variant (App. E.5) claims a ~10x training-compute win over
  Huginn's truncated BPTT from ONE config with no depth curve.
- The clean-room testbed (`/home/wolfe/11-DiffusionBlocks-Testing`) is a faithful port
  (byte-exact against the authors' released modules; the fidelity audit found the one
  bug in the AUTHORS' code, an outer-product loss weight, and fixing it makes DB worse).
  On held-out CE at σ_max the faithful four-block arm is 0.67 nats behind AR at 4x the
  tokens and still closing; B=1 is DB's weakest partition; the soft bridge collapses
  ‖D̂‖ to 0.27 and the argmax bridge fixes generation (gen-PPL 596 → 17); sampling
  after the ladder is a point mass; the σ-grid mean ranks arms backwards.
- Every DiffusionBlocks-on-the-loop arm in MORPH was depth-dead or depth-inverted
  (dbfix K=1 4.4652 → K=8 4.5101), and none of them carried an explicit denoising loss.
- Every flow-matching planner that wrote a MEAN-POOLED span latent into an additive
  prefix scored a plan worth of 0.0000, even when the TRUE target was planted: the
  target carried nothing decodable, and a zero-parameter copy heuristic matched a
  trained 22M-parameter planner. The rejection note leaves one cell open: an FM
  objective on the model's own PROCESSED states.
- The `n_core: 0` model WITH the packed slot row runs on the current tree (smoke
  2026-09-03, exit 0); the old slot-only core, `iter_cond.py` and `step_mix` do not.

## Proposal

One shared backbone, one control, two dmorph arms. Everything on branch `feat/dmorph`,
work tree `/home/wolfe/morph-dmorph`. Nothing here touches the shipped paid-loop path:
`dmorph.enabled: false` builds NO new parameter and the forward is bit-identical.

### Backbone (all three arms)

`MORPHConfig(n_prelude=6, n_core=0, n_coda=6)` — a 12-layer flat stack, the paper's
AR depth and a clean 4-block partition; 241.7M params at d=1024 (A2 is 265.1M at 47
passes/token; this is 13.4). The packed TUL row is ON from step 0 (`tul.activate_at:
0.0`, `slot_seed: boundary`, `emit_weight: 0.0`, `plast_weight: 1.0`,
`token_state_dropout: 0.15`, `prefix_k: 2`, the panel's seq 1024 / batch 6 / 20k
steps). The CLEAN stream is exactly the shipped forward: `_tul_front` → the 12 layers →
`_back_region` → token CE. Its head is the primary metric on every arm, so the coreless
bar and both dmorph arms are compared on the same head.

### The noisy stream (shared machinery)

A second stream over the same packed row, at flow time `t ~ U(0,1)` drawn per row
(one `t` per sample; a per-position `t` is a config switch, default off — one `t` per
row keeps one block per sample, which is what makes the routing pay), with the
rectified-flow path

    x_t = (1 - t) · x_0 + t · y,        x_0 ~ N(0, s² I),  s = 1/sqrt(d)   (matched source)
    v*  = y - x_0

`y` is unit-L2 (the FM-planner lesson; never `SliceScaler`). The stream is routed
through ONE block of the flat stack chosen by `t`: `B = 4` equal-width bands on `t`
(uniform `t` makes equal-width equal-mass), `t ∈ [0, 1/4)` (mostly noise, coarse
work) → block 0 = layers 0–2, …, `t ∈ [3/4, 1)` → block 3 = layers 9–11. This is the
paper's σ→block reversal in `t` coordinates (high σ ↔ low t ↔ early block). Overlap:
`gamma: 0.1` widening of each band, as the paper.

Inside its block the noisy stream runs the SAME layers and weights as the clean stream,
with two differences: (1) time conditioning through AdaLN-Zero on each layer of the
block — port `CoreStageConditioning` from `git show d9e04e6:morph/model/iter_cond.py`
(zero-init modulation, bit-identical at init) keyed by `t` through
`SigmaConditioning` (`morph/model/diffusion_blocks.py`); (2) attention: every noisy
query at position `i` attends the CLEAN stream's keys and values at positions `j ≤ i`
from the same layer, and nothing else. That is the paper's concatenated-sequence mask
without the 2L sequence: the clean pass captures its K/V bundle per layer
(`cla_capture`, already implemented in `morph/model/attention.py`) and the noisy pass
consumes it (`cla_kv`). The clean stream NEVER sees the noisy stream, so token CE
cannot leak the target (port the perturbation proof `tests/test_no_leak.py` from the
testbed, positive control included). The documented deviation from the paper's mask —
the noisy query does not attend other noisy keys — is kept and named.

Output: a velocity head `v̂ = W_v · h_out` (zero-init, so the stream starts at "no
velocity") and the x0 estimate `D̂ = x_t + (1 - t) · v̂`. Loss terms, all logged per
group in `out[...]` like `_tul_group_losses`:

    L = CE_clean + λ_fm · mean_valid ‖v̂ - v*‖²  +  λ_ce · CE(head(D̂), label)

`λ_fm` uses the FM planner's `loss_scale: auto` convention (divide by the analytic null
floor so the term starts near 1 and does not drown a 4–11 nat CE). The CE term through
the tied head from `D̂` is the testbed's Option B — the only DB variant that ever used
context. `dmorph.detach_ctx: false` by default (the paper's one-network gradient); the
switch exists because the FM arc's detach rule is the first thing a reviewer will ask
about.

### Arm dm-tok — next-token embedding at every position (the paper's AR DiffusionBlocks, FM-parameterized)

`y_i = normalize(embed(label_i))` at every position with a label (`labels != -100`;
slot prefix positions carry no label and are masked out of both terms). The noisy stream
covers the whole row. At inference each new token runs the clean pass (as today, its
head is always available) and then `B` Euler steps of the noisy stream at the last
position, one block per step in order 0→3, `t` on the ascending equal-width grid, with
the HARD bridge (argmax row of the embedding table) between steps; the final `D̂` is
read out through the head. The clean head and the ladder head are BOTH scored.

### Arm dm-hs — hidden-state diffusion at the slot positions

The noisy stream lives only at slot positions (v1 runs it on full-row tensors with
non-slot positions masked out of the loss — the per-slot gather is an optimization,
not a correctness question). `y_s = normalize(stopgrad(h_s^final))`, the CLEAN
stream's own post-stack state at slot `s` (after `_back_region`'s final norm, before
the head): the "post-core carriers" cell the FM rejection left open, with the target
detached so the model cannot collapse it (a live target with `sigreg` is a config
switch, default off). The noisy stream's block is asked to reach the 12-layer state
from noise in 3 layers of its own compute plus the clean K/V it attends. At inference
the `B`-step ladder produces a refined slot state; it is scored by (a) its cosine to
the clean target and (b) the plan-worth protocol: CE of the NEXT span's tokens with the
slot state = clean (the shipped path) versus = ladder output versus = zero versus =
shuffled-other-row (`lab/divergence/slot_path_worth.py`'s protocol, rebuilt for this
path). Report the COST, never a fraction (probing doctrine rule 1).

### Control

The same backbone and config with `dmorph.enabled: false`: the coreless bar on this
depth. It must be re-run — the 4:0:4 bar (4.3102) is a different network.

## Alternatives considered

- **Paper-literal one-block-per-optimizer-step with `B`x the steps.** Rejected for v1:
  that protocol buys memory we do not need on a 128–265M model and costs the paper's
  4x step count; routing by a per-sample `t` gives every block gradient in every step at
  1.25x the control's FLOPs. It is one config switch away if v2 wants the memory.
- **Mean-pooled next-span target at the slot (the FM planner's `y`).** Rejected: the
  oracle probe scored 0.0000 worth with the TRUE target planted; the target is empty.
  dm-hs takes the model's own processed state instead (the open cell); dm-tok takes the
  paper's decodable-by-construction embedding target.
- **Restoring the slot-only core and `dbfix` for a K-sweep.** Rejected: every one of
  those arms was depth-dead or inverted and carried no denoising loss; the record is at
  `d9e04e6` and repeating it answers nothing.
- **A separate planner network (FM1).** Rejected: 22M parameters that never beat a copy
  heuristic; dmorph shares the backbone's weights and gives the FM gradient to them.
- **Whole-sequence diffusion (SEDD / MD4).** Out of scope; the slot geometry is the
  part of MORPH being kept.
- **Scoring on MAUVE / teacher gen-PPL.** Refused: that is the metric gap the paper's
  parity claim lives in (greedy DB decoding against a top-p baseline). Token CE on the
  clean head decides; generation numbers travel with the diversity guard.

## Acceptance criteria

1. `dmorph.enabled: false` is bit-identical to the current tree (loss, every parameter,
   every gradient) — `tests/test_dmorph_off_is_bit_identical.py`.
2. No leak: perturbing the noisy stream's input, or any label, moves NO clean-head logit
   (exact zero), with a positive control that a clean-stream perturbation does —
   `tests/test_dmorph_no_leak.py`.
3. The band→block map is one function used by training, eval and the generator; `t` in
   band `b` runs exactly layers `[3b, 3b+3)` and nothing else — asserted by a layer-call
   spy.
4. The FM loss at `t → 1` on a zero-velocity head equals the analytic null floor;
   `D̂ = x_t + (1 - t) v̂` recovers `y` exactly when `v̂ = v*`.
5. The hard bridge returns embedding rows of full norm; the ladder with `B` steps calls
   each block exactly once, in order.
6. dm-hs: the target is detached (no gradient reaches the clean stream through `y`),
   and the worth scorer's four conditions (clean / ladder / zero / shuffled) run on the
   shipped path and produce four distinct numbers on a random model.
7. `dmorph_smoke.yaml` runs end to end on the 5090 for both arms and the control;
   `pytest tests/` green on CPU; `python scripts/verify_template.py` ok.
8. A frozen prereg exists before any panel run
   (`lab/experiments/failures/2026-09-03-dmorph-v1-panel.md`, predictions written
   before results).

## Risks

- **The σ*/autoencoding trap in `t` clothing.** At high `t` the noisy input nearly IS
  the target and the CE term through `D̂` is trivial. Measure the training-mass fraction
  above the decodability threshold (port `scripts/decodability.py`'s test) before
  trusting any dmorph loss curve; if it exceeds ~40 %, reshape the `t` distribution and
  say so in the filing.
- **The clean head may not move at all.** The noisy stream's gradient reaches the shared
  weights, but nothing forces the clean head to benefit; the prereg's P1 is exactly
  this and a null is a real answer.
- **Eager attention.** The `cla_kv` path may fall back to the windowed reference
  attention; the wall-clock axis then measures kernels, not architecture. Report
  `flop_proxy` next to tok/s every time.
- **n=1 is unreadable** (6.5 % median spread): two seeds per arm minimum; score on the
  480-row same-rows depth sweep or the last-20 eval mean, never on one eval.

### Deviations from the Proposal, and choices the Proposal left open

1. **The cross-stream window includes key `i`.** The `cla_kv` path inherited XSA's
   `j < i` window (self-exclusion is a clean-path property: the query's own value is
   added back separately). For the noisy query the paper's mask is `j ≤ i` on the clean
   stream, and without key `i` the noisy query could not see the clean current token at
   all at init (the `x0` injection gate is zero). `_window_attn(..., exclude_self=False)`
   on both `cla_kv` branches is the one `attention.py` change, forced by the
   reachability half of the leak test.
2. **Noisy → noisy channels exist, and are causal.** The Proposal names "no noisy → noisy
   attention" as the deviation from the paper's 2L mask. That holds for attention; it
   does not make the stream position-isolated. CCA's causal conv on the query (two
   stacked kernel-4 convs per layer, reach 6 positions) and the GLA scan on retention
   layers both carry the noisy state forward in position. Both are strictly causal, so
   a noisy query at `i` can read noisy `j < i`, whose target is a PAST token.
   `test_x_t_reaches_later_noisy_positions_only_through_causal_channels` pins the
   direction and the conv reach.
3. **K/V is captured on all 12 layers on every clean pass**, not only on the sampled
   block's layers. Deterministic memory and a compile-stable capture; the cost is the
   K/V of 9 unused layers per step (bf16 activations, not weights).
4. **Block first, then `t`.** Training draws the block uniformly (`block_visit` reshapes
   it, the P6 lever), then `t` uniform in the γ-widened band. Uniform `t` then band would
   give the same marginal without the lever.
5. **Targets are detached in both arms.** dm-tok: `normalize(lm_weight[label])`,
   detached (the head is weight-tied; a live target trains the input table through the
   FM loss). dm-hs: `normalize(stopgrad(h))` as proposed; live only under
   `sigreg_lambda > 0`, which raises for the tok arm.
6. **The noisy stream gets the clean stream's injections.** `x0` injection, bigram and
   the bagged value embedding are re-applied on the noisy carrier, keyed by the clean
   CURRENT token (never the label; the leak test covers it). It gets no token-state
   dropout and no `input_norm`; `x_t` enters scaled by `in_gain = sqrt(d)` so a unit
   target lands at the carrier's scale.
7. **Conditioning.** `CoreStageConditioning` was not ported as a file; its mechanism is:
   `SigmaConditioning`-style sinusoidal embedding of `t` (`t_embed_scale`), a 256-wide
   MLP, and one zero-init AdaLN gate per layer (`AdaLNGate` from `diffusion_blocks.py`),
   so the stream is bit-identical to the plain block at init.
8. **Velocity head and read-out scale.** `v̂ = W_v · AdaLN(RMSNorm(h))` with `W_v`
   zero-init (the DiT final layer). The tied head reads `D̂` through a learnable scalar
   `head_scale` (init `sqrt(d)`, no decay) in place of the testbed's closed-form scale;
   at init a unit-norm `D̂` scores like a carrier of the table's scale.
9. **CE through `D̂` in both arms** (`lambda_ce: 1.0`). dm-tok scores the clean CE's
   positions with the clean row weights (`plast 1.0 / emit 0.0`); dm-hs scores the emit
   positions, the one place a slot state is read.
10. **dm-hs worth is the emit-position CE through the head** with the slot state set to
    the target (clean), the ladder output, zero, or another row's state at the same slot
    index. A flat stack has no reader of a POST-stack slot state except the head, so the
    worth protocol cannot re-run a coda on a substituted state as
    `slot_path_worth.py` does; a coda-ENTRY target (block 3's input) is the v2 option
    that would make that protocol apply.
11. **Per-position `t`** (`t_per_position: true`) runs every block on every row and
    masks per position: B× the block cost, not 1.25×. Default off, unit-tested only.
12. **Eval is a fixed instrument.** Source noise and ladder noise come from a seed-0
    generator; `t` is a stratified grid rotated per batch by a phase derived from the
    rows (`eval_phase`), because the first smoke showed a fixed grid pins each stratum
    to one band (`dm_n_band0 = 0` at batch 2); per-band and masked terms are
    count-weighted across the eval set (`aggregate_eval`; a band with no row is NaN,
    never 0).
13. **The σ*-trap is visible at init**, as the Risks predicted: on the smoke's tiny vocab
    `dm_ce_band1 = 7.15` against `dm_ce_band3 = 0.41`, and `dm_target_ce = 0.015` (a
    decodable-by-construction target). `lab/dmorph/decodability.py` gives the
    training-mass fraction above the decodability threshold; run it before trusting a
    loss curve.
14. `_tul_front` returns a 4-tuple; three call sites in `tests/test_tul_fm1.py` were
    updated (that file is otherwise untouched: 25 passed).

  vocabulary, 12 steps) `val/dm_ce` is 11.03 for hs against 3.77 for tok, because the
  head cannot read a random unit state, while a tok target is decodable by
  construction. `val/loss` excludes the dm terms on every arm.

### The σ*-trap, measured at init (`lab/dmorph/decodability.py`, d=1024, V=49169, n=4096, threshold 0.5)

The Risks section pre-committed a response if the training mass above the decodability
threshold exceeded ~40 %. It is 89 % with the Proposal's matched source. Nearest-neighbour
accuracy of `x_t` against the embedding table, and the fraction of block-first training
mass above the first `t` that decodes at ≥ 0.5:

| `source_std` | ‖x₀‖ / ‖y‖ | t* | mass above t* | band of t* |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1/32 (matched, the Proposal) | 1.0 | 0.125 | **0.886** | 0 |
| 0.0625 | 2.0 | 0.225 | 0.796 | 0 |
| 0.125 | 4.0 | 0.350 | 0.646 | 1 |
| 0.25 | 8.0 | 0.525 | 0.458 | 2 |
| 0.5 | 16.0 | 0.700 | 0.313 | 2 |
| 1.0 | 32.0 | 0.825 | 0.159 | 3 |
| 2.0 | 64.0 | 0.900 | 0.091 | 3 |

Why the matched source fails here and did not in the FM planner: decodability is a max
over 49k rows of `⟨x_t, e_v⟩`; at unit ‖x₀‖ in 1024 dimensions the noise projection onto
any row is `O(1/√d)`, so a `t` of 0.13 already separates the target row from the rest.
The planner's target was undecodable for a different reason (a bag-mean). Under the
Proposal's setting three of four blocks train on inputs that already name their token,
and the CE-through-D̂ term is trivial for them (the smoke's `dm_ce_band3 = 0.41` against
`dm_ce_band1 = 7.15`). This is the DiffusionBlocks σ_max lesson in `t` coordinates
(`research/2026-09-03-db-testbed-ladder.md`, "σ_max is the metric").

Not changed by the build: the panel configs keep `source_std: matched`, because the
prereg Method says "build per the design note" and its Predictions are locked. The
decision for the launcher: amend Method (dated reason) to `dmorph.source_std=0.5` or
`1.0` on both dmorph arms, which puts t* in band 2–3 and 16–31 % of the mass above it,
at the price of a mismatched source (`null_floor` becomes 257 / 1025; `loss_scale:
auto` divides it out, and `in_gain` still scales `x_t` by √d). `block_visit` cannot do
this alone: t* sits inside band 0 at the matched source. The hs arm's target is a unit
hidden state, not a table row, so this table does not transfer to it; its trap is the
head's CE through `D̂`, read the same way per band.

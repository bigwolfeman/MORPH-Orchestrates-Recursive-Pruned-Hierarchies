# Paper note: LoopMTP, looped transformer with latent multi-token prediction

- **Title:** LoopMTP: A looped transformer guided by latent multi-token prediction (Shomali, Frey, Berghaus, Koehler, Ali; Lamarr Institute, Bonn)
- **arXiv or URL:** 2608.03624 (v1, 2026-08-04, cs.CL). Type: paper.
- **Local paper text:** docs/references/looping-depth/loopmtp-2608-03624/ (paper.txt, paper.pdf, SOURCE.md)
- **Date first noted:** 2026-10-20

## 1. Why we reference it

- 2026-10-20: It is a pass-indexed auxiliary target (pass t aligned to the token t steps ahead) plus a learned gate that sums all pass outputs. Our rule is "only the main loss buys depth" and our memory records a LoopMTP slot port at -0.0001 vs control ("horizon targets degenerate on web text"). The gated aggregation and per-pass norms are separable ideas and were not in that port.
- 2026-10-20: Full text read through Sec. 5 (main results, MTP ablation, gate ablation, GSM8K expert). Appendices (training details, FLOPs table) only grepped.

## 2. What the paper shows

- 2026-10-20 (Sec. 3): the whole 12-layer model is looped (d 1024, 32 heads), with an iteration-index scalar, per-iteration LayerNorm, input fusion by concat+projection of re-normalized token embedding and previous state, and a 1/T residual scale ("Loop-LNS"). Pass outputs are combined by z = sum_t g_t * x_t with softplus gates (one d x d linear plus T biases, <0.5 % params), initialized so pass 1 dominates. Loss = NTP + lambda_align * mean_t (1 - cos(x_i^(t), sg[E u_{i+t}])) for t>=2 + a KL-to-uniform ponder term on the gates.
- 2026-10-20 (Table 1, 6.8B tokens Nemotron-CC, Muon+AdamW, 3 seeds): LoopMTP 260M vs non-looped 266M: OpenWebText PPL 20.92 to 21.57 across T=3..9 vs 23.56; FineWebEdu 18.86 to 19.43 vs 21.08; general-task average 50.02 vs 46.28. LoopFormer (280M) is worse than LoopMTP and diverged in one of three seeds at T=3.
- 2026-10-20 (Sec. 4.3, Fig. 2): without the MTP term, math BPB degrades monotonically with more loops; with it, the second pass already maps to a distinct subspace (cosine to pass-1 output much lower). Median rank of the ground-truth token at trained offsets is up to 35.6x better with MTP (Fig. 3).
- 2026-10-20 (Sec. 4.4, Fig. 4): gate ablation: learnable gates beat last-pass-only, learnable last-pass-only, and uniform average, with the gap widest on math BPB at high T. Mean gate rises with depth (0.17 to 0.26 at T=5).
- 2026-10-20 (Sec. 5): domain expert on Nemotron-CC-Math, GSM8K 8-shot 19.03 % vs 7.05 % for the non-looped model at equal params. Some loop counts hurt more samples than they rescue.
- Failure modes: BPB regresses slightly at T=9; compute is NOT matched (Appendix D: 2.49x to 7.05x the training and inference FLOPs of the non-looped model for T=3..9, 0.59 to 0.60x the training FLOPs of LoopFormer).

## 3. Our speculation

- 2026-10-20 Speculation: the headline gain over the non-looped baseline is confounded by compute (T passes on all 12 layers vs one pass) and the baseline's smaller FFN; the paper never reports a K1-K6-like same-model depth curve with and without the aux term, only across separately trained models.
- 2026-10-20 Speculation: their sharpened token rank per offset shows the aux term works as intended on the aligned pass, but a per-pass aux target is the banned form for us. Gated aggregation is the part with no aux loss and could be tested on its own.
- 2026-10-20 Speculation: gate-init prior to pass 1 (acts like non-looped at start) resembles our ramp plus injection floor: begin near identity-to-input.

## 5. Where our findings disagree

- 2026-10-20: They find MTP-style latent targets help a small looped LM at 6.8B tokens (and unlike Gloeckle MTP at small scale). We measured a slot port at -0.0001 on 5k OWT steps. Different token budget (6.8B vs 31M), different loop (full stack vs core), so not a clean conflict; our memory "horizon targets degenerate on web text" is the 5k reading.

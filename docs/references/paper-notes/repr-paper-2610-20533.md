# Paper note: RePR (learned latents from PMI)

- **Title:** RePR, learning latents by regressing onto PMI (Baade et al.), with Latent Forcing
- **arXiv or URL:** arXiv 2610.20533. Code: github.com/AlanBaade/repr
- **Local paper text:** none archived
- **Date first noted:** 2026-10-15

## 1. Why we reference it

- 2026-10-15: Our LCM and span NCM codes were trained with reconstruction. RePR offers a self-supervised way to define the span "thought" target.

## 2. What the paper shows

Read in full on 2026-10-15. Section numbers not recorded.

- 2026-10-15 (representation target): Scalar PMI increments, estimated by the generative model itself. The target is stop-gradient log p(z|x,y) minus log p(z|y). For images it uses the diffusion ELBO. No negatives and no codebook. The paper says it resists feature suppression.
- 2026-10-15 (telescoping): Per-token increments that sum to the sequence total (eqs. 13-15).
- 2026-10-15 (loss weight): A small loss weight of 0.005 works best. R is trained every 4th step.
- 2026-10-15 (iteration): Iterating (predicting the last iteration's features) gives large gains.
- 2026-10-15 (Latent Forcing): One model denoises latents first, then pixels. The method is lossless. The decoder sees noised latents.

## 3. Our speculation

- 2026-10-15 Speculation: A PMI target could define a span code without a reconstruction loss. This is untested on our models.

## 5. Where our findings disagree

- 2026-10-15: Our measurement: on text, the representation heads did not fit the PMI target. R squared on validation is -0.02. Paper's claim: the PMI target works as a representation target (section 2 above). Our notes record no text result from the paper. Source: Parcae run ncm-repr-tok-5k (branch lxtul/ncm-repr).

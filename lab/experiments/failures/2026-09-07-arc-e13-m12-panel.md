## Question

Every stable slot loop so far was trained at a Poisson mean of 6 and does its work by
iteration 3 (half its mean; the normal shape for a looped model). E6 showed that a plain
loop trained at a deeper draw depends on its depth (K3−K6 0.277 at mean 16), E7 showed the
mask arm at mean 16 (max 24, grad through 8) detonates under the hinge alone, and E12's
one run showed the mask arm at a FIXED depth of 12 under full BPTT enters E7's
power-iteration mode by step 900 (first-iteration state scale x170, hinge blind).
With the fixed-point term on the slot loop and the per-slot draw at Poisson mean 12 (max
16, full BPTT so no sample loses its gradient window), what does each TUL variant do with
a mean of 12 iterations, does it survive, what does it cost, and do the tokens read it?

## Verdict

Failure on the panel's main predictions (P13a, P13b's K6−K12 half, P13c, P13d). A Poisson
draw of mean 12 is safe for the three loss shapes that were safe at mean 6 (no E7 mode: the
first-iteration ratio stays at 1.0 under the hinge; only the fixed depth of E12 and the MTP
heads open it) and it changes what the loop CARRIES, not how deep it works: on the masked
arm the loop's share of the tokens' work grows 2.3x (K1−K6 0.021 → 0.049) and of the
forecast 3x, at the SAME end point as mean 6 (mask@6 − E4@6 = -0.0008 [-0.0031, +0.0015]); past
iteration 3 nothing moves (+0.0018 [+0.0016, +0.0020] tokens). Without the mask the loop is a free
ride at either draw, and A1 without any forecast head has the best token CE of the panel
(4.141 vs 4.221 vs 4.335): the MUX loss costs the tokens 0.08 nats and the mask 0.11 more.
The MTP heads at weight 1.0 on the masked slot loop detonate it in the scale mode by 2138.

## Updated hypothesis

Depth dependence on the slot loop scales with the draw (E6 on the plain loop, now here) and
is not value: the loop takes over work the prelude did. The lever for value is what the slot
is asked to compute (E3, staged targets) or where its output is written (the Spiral note,
parked), not the draw's mean. The hinge's typical-gain bound is scale-blind: the fixed depth
and the heads both opened the E7 mode under it, so the divergence README's regime table
needs a row for "first-iteration scale growth under a passing hinge" with `loop/core_gain_t0`
as the instrument (it led the tripwire by ~600 steps on mtp4, ~500 on E12). MTP heads on
the TUL coda need a weight far below 1.0 or a warmup before they can be read.

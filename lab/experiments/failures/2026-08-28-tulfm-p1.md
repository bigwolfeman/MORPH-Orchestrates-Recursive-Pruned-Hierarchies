# Planned: can a flow-matching objective write span-(i+1) content into a latent at all?

Status: failure — R1/R2 missed their bars in both pre-registered runs; P1b wrote real but under-threshold conditional content
Arc: [`.agents/notes/proposed/architecture/2026-08-28-tul-fm-arc.md`](../../../.agents/notes/proposed/architecture/2026-08-28-tul-fm-arc.md)
Doctrine: [`docs/tul-fm-probing.md`](../../../docs/tul-fm-probing.md) §3, §5
Code: `lab/tulfm/{fm_planner,train_p1,retrieval_probe}.py`, `morph/configs/tulfm_p1.yaml`
Gate suite: `tests/test_tulfm_p1.py` (28 tests, CPU)

## Question

The 2026-08 campaign decomposed TUL's failure into WRITE, DYNAMICS and READ. Finding 1
was that the main CE writes essentially nothing into the plan latent: the specificity 2x2
read 65.1 % / 0.4 % / 3.0 % / 0.3 %, and only the aux losses put span-specific content
into `z`. P1 asks the narrowest possible replacement question:

> With the backbone FROZEN and no core loop in the path at all, can a flow-matching
> (EDM denoising) objective write the content of span *i+1* into a latent that is
> conditioned only on positions `<= e_i`?

It is not a CE question and no CE number is reported. The gate is retrieval.

## Hypothesis

A denoising regression onto the pooled representation of the NEXT span, trained
single-pass at one sampled σ per slot, writes span-specific content that a cosine
retrieval probe can recover well above chance — because the objective's ONLY way to
lower its loss in the high-σ bands is to predict `y_{i+1}` from the context, whereas
CE-through-the-coda always has the free token path to lower it instead.

The competing hypothesis this must beat is **document identification**: rows come from
different documents, so a planner that learns only "which document is this" already
beats batch-wide chance. That is why the gate is written on the WITHIN-ROW score.

## Method

* **Frozen backbone:** `checkpoints/morph/a3-s2/step_4500.pt`, architecture and span rule
  from `tul_a3` (`n_core: 0`, `tul.activate_at: never`). Every backbone parameter is set
  `requires_grad=False` and the freeze is asserted twice — once on the module, once
  against the optimizer's own parameter set.
* **Features:** `Hpre = input_norm(prelude(input_ids))` with the HC streams meaned — the
  exact tensor the A3 coda consumes. The SAME tensor supplies the conditioning and the
  pooled targets. Read through the new `MORPHTransformer.prelude_states` hook, which
  refuses to run in `train()` mode.
* **Spans:** `BoundaryRule.cut` via `build_boundary_rule` — the one rule the TUL loader
  and generator use. Slot `i` ends at boundary `p_i`; its target is the CLOSED span
  `[p_i+1, p_{i+1}]`. The trailing open span is never a target.
* **Targets:** mean-pooled `Hpre` over the target span, then **unit L2 norm**. Not
  per-component-std (`SliceScaler`) — that is the scar that pushed σ* to 3.3.
* **Planner:** 21.94 M params, `d_p=512`, 4 pre-norm layers, 8 heads, SwiGLU `d_ff=1408`,
  AdaLN-Zero σ conditioning. Slot self-attention causal in slot order; cross-attention to
  the context masked to positions `<= e_i`. ONE stack, shared across every σ and every
  Euler step; training never iterates it.
* **Objective:** `σ ~ LogNormal(-1.2, 1.2)` restricted to `[0.002, 80]` and renormalised
  in CDF space (audited `DBSchedule`, `mode='b1'`), per slot independently;
  `loss = mean_valid[ w(σ)·‖D̂ − y‖² ]` with the audited EDM `w` and `σ_data = 0.5`.
* **Inference (probe only):** `z ~ N(0, (1+σ_max²)I)`, 6 Euler steps down the
  equi-probability descending ladder, then one final read at σ_min.
* **Run:** 4000 steps, batch 8, seq_len 1024, AdamW lr 3e-4 (200 warmup, cosine to 0.1x),
  wd 0.01, grad clip 1.0, bf16 autocast with fp32 grads, seed 0, wandb `morph-tul`.
* **Probe:** `lab/tulfm/retrieval_probe.py` on 8 held-out batches (loader skips 100 000
  documents), reporting BOTH candidate scopes and both controls in one invocation.

## Predictions (frozen 2026-08-28, before the planner has taken a single GPU step)

**R1 — batch-wide retrieval clears the document cue.** Trained top-1 ≥ **0.06** on
held-out rows at step 4000. That is ~26x chance and ~5x the measured document-cue-only
baseline. Below 0.03 (2x the document cue) is not a pass, whatever the multiple of chance
looks like.

**R2 — within-row retrieval, the sharp gate.** Trained within-row top-1 ≥ **0.06**
(chance ≈ 0.0187, so ≥ 3.2x) and within-row MRR ≥ **0.12**. This is the number the
verdict is written on. The document cue scores exactly chance here by construction, so
anything above chance is span-level content.

**R3 — the untrained control sits at the floor.** A freshly initialised planner scores
top-1 ≤ 2x chance in BOTH scopes. Its output head is zero-init, so the ladder is a pure
noise walk and this is a defined floor, not a hope. If R3 fails the probe is broken and
no other number in this file may be read (doctrine §5).

**R4 — the shuffled-context control collapses.** Conditioning slot `i` on another row's
`Hpre` drops within-row top-1 to ≤ 1.5x chance AND to ≤ 0.4x the trained score. If the
shuffle costs nothing, the planner learned a corpus prior, not this row's context.

**R5 — the targets do not move.** Effective rank stays in 20-30 of 1024, mean pairwise
cosine in 0.58-0.64, `‖y‖ = 1.0000`, dropped fraction ≤ 0.03, at every eval. The backbone
is frozen, so these are CONSTANTS: a drift is a bug in the pipeline, not target gaming.
Target gaming is a P2/P3 risk and cannot occur here.

**R6 — the high-σ bands actually learn.** With 6 equi-probability bands, band 5
(σ ∈ [0.96, 80]) reaches `rel = loss / null_floor` ≤ **0.85** by step 4000, while band 0
(σ ∈ [0.002, 0.094]) stays ≥ **0.98**. Band 0 is unlearnable by construction — its
preconditioned target is `−ε` — so a fall there would mean a leak, and a flat band 5
would mean the objective never reached the prediction regime.

**R7 — no train/val gap in the objective.** Held-out `rel_loss` within 0.02 of the
training `rel_loss` at step 4000. 22 M params against ~430 slots per step over 4000 steps
should not memorise, and if it does the retrieval number is about memorisation.

## Decision rule

| Outcome | Verdict |
|---|---|
| R2 and R4 hold (R3 held) | **P1 GATES.** The objective writes span-(i+1) content. Proceed to P2 (wire the coda under token scarcity). |
| R1 holds, R2 fails | The planner learned the DOCUMENT, not the span. NOT a pass. Report the gap; P2 is not justified on this evidence. |
| R1 and R2 fail, R6 holds | The objective trains but writes nothing retrievable. Sweep `sigma.sigma_data` to `1/√d = 0.031` (the value that balances the two EDM limits for unit-norm targets — see the σ_data section of `lab/tulfm/fm_planner.py`) and re-run ONCE. If that also fails, the arc note moves to `rejected/` and the DB line is closed on MORPH. |
| R6 fails (band 5 rel ≥ 0.98) | The objective never reached the prediction regime. The `sigma_data` sweep is MANDATORY before any verdict is written. |
| R3 fails | The probe is broken. Fix the probe; no verdict. |

## What this experiment cannot decide

* **Whether the coda would READ such a plan.** That is P2. P1 measures writing only.
* **Whether a co-trained target would stay honest.** The backbone is frozen, so target
  gaming is structurally impossible here and SIGReg is not exercised.
* **Anything about CE.** No CE number is produced and none should be quoted from this run.
* **Anything about the takeover.** There is no core loop in this path (`n_core: 0`) and no
  BPTT through an iterated map. The dynamics finding is untouched, not refuted.

The OOD-shock caveat from the arc note's risk list (the `seed_bagmean` B2 precedent) does
NOT apply: P1 forces no new input into the frozen backbone. It only reads states the
backbone already produces for ordinary token rows.

## Results (filled 2026-08-28, runs tulfm-p1 / xc6msgbf and tulfm-p1b / w9fb4wjr)

Both runs: 4000 steps, ~3.2 min each on the 5090, frozen `a3-s2/step_4500` (348/348
tensors), batch 8, seed 0. Probe JSONs: `results/2026-08-28-tulfm-p1/probe.json`,
`probe_p1b.json`.

**P1 primary (sigma_data = 0.5):**

| condition | top1 (batch, chance .0025) | top1 (within-row, chance .0201) | MRR (wr) |
|---|---|---|---|
| trained | 0.0050 | 0.0235 | 0.0946 |
| untrained | 0.0028 | 0.0226 | 0.0911 |
| shuffled ctx | 0.0021 | 0.0225 | 0.0911 |

All bands sat at rel ~= 0.51 — the planner learned the UNCONDITIONAL prior (mean +
rank-25 anisotropy) and nothing conditional. Band 0 fell to 0.537 against the ">= 0.98"

reading: live-subspace rel 1.018 (nothing learned along the 25 live dims), dead-subspace
rel 0.717. The prediction's "unlearnable by construction" derivation assumed isotropic
targets; the real targets are rank ~25 of 1024.

**P1b fallback (sigma_data = 1/sqrt(d) = 0.03125, the pre-registered re-run):**

Nulls balanced at ~1024 in every band as predicted; rels 0.27-0.38 everywhere; R7 held

## Verdict: FAILURE (predictions did not hold)

- R1 failed twice (0.0050, then 0.0153 vs >= 0.06; the 0.0153 sits AT the document-cue
  baseline band 0.009-0.015).
- R2 — the gate — failed twice on top-1 (0.0235, then 0.0423 vs >= 0.06). The MRR half
  passed in P1b (0.1336 >= 0.12); the compound still fails as written.
- R4 held in P1b in spirit and nearly to the letter: shuffled context collapses to
  0.0182 (<= 1.5x chance ✓; 0.43x trained vs the <= 0.4x line — a hair over).
- R6's band-0 clause was WRONG as derived (anisotropy, not leak); band-5 clause held in
  both runs.

Per the frozen decision rule this closes P1: the arc note is due for `rejected/`.
The rule's premise, however, was "writes nothing retrievable", and P1b measurably does
NOT satisfy that premise: trained 2.1x chance within-row with BOTH controls at chance,
and the signal dies when the context is shuffled — genuine span-(i+1), context-conditional
content, the first any objective has written into a MORPH plan latent. Under-threshold,
not absent. The decision on the arc note is escalated rather than executed silently;
see Updated hypothesis.

## Updated hypothesis

The FM objective CAN write context-conditional next-span content into z with no BPTT
and no takeover exposure; at 4000 steps / 22M params / raw anisotropic targets it writes
~2x chance, not the 3x+ the gate demanded. The named, untested confound is target
ANISOTROPY: sigma_data rescaling cannot fix a rank-25-of-1024 target geometry (the
subspace diagnostic shows the planner spends its capacity on the dead dims). The clean
fix — whiten (optionally rank-truncate) the frozen targets so sigma_data = 1 holds
per-component — was proposed BEFORE either run's probe was read (conversation record,
2026-08-28) and is the natural P1c, as a NEW pre-registration with its own frozen
thresholds. What P1b already settles for the arc: the no-BPTT training rule trains
stably (no takeover mechanism exists to fire), the bands balance exactly as the EDM
arithmetic says, and the probe instrument resolves 2x-chance effects cleanly.

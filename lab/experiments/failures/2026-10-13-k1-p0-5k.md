# Flat K/V kernels (K1) and PDL (P0) on the speed base at 5k

Status: failure

## Question

`model.kv_flat_kernels` (K1, merge d47486ef) re-tiles the CCA conv backward and the prologue rows
backward and sums the weight-grad partials in one fixed-order kernel. It changes the sum order,
so it is rounding only: gradient against key off, global relative difference 3.95e-3, which is
the bf16 error of either path against fp64. Pinned bench 164.12 -> 160.20 ms. `model.pdl` (P0)
prints the same byte-gate hash on and off and saves 0.5-0.95 ms. Does the speed base with both
keys on train the same function at 5k?

## Hypothesis

Both keys change rounding or timing only. The arm sits inside the seed spread of the G1 runs.

### Split (amended method, 2026-10-13, after the failure)

`abq/chain-kvsplit.sh` on the same tree, log `abq/chain-kvsplit.log`:

K1 alone is clean to step 600.

## Verdict

Failure. P0 (`model.pdl`) breaks training on the Poisson speed base, and neither byte gate sees it:
the base gate and the speed-config gate are both bit-exact with the key on. K1 is not implicated
by the split, but its 5k quality is not measured here (every 5k run had P0 on).

## Updated hypothesis

PDL changes the result only off the gated path: a variable cell count (M below 4 draws new graphs),
a step past 45, or the training schedule after the LR ramp starts. P0 is rejected: its saving
(0.46-0.95 ms, at the 0.5 ms keep line) is not worth a hazard that the gate cannot see. Lesson for
every timing-only change: a 45-step deterministic byte gate at M = 4 does not prove a launch-order
change safe; run the 600-step stability read on the shipped base too. Next: K1 alone at 5k
(`planned/2026-10-13-k1-5k.md`), and remove the P0 code.

## Updated hypothesis

The round-1 story ("the mask+MUX write INVERTED the price of restriction") was
wrong. The corrected story, from these three arms:

1. **MUX is the CE lever, mask or no mask.** Unmasked+mux beats everything
   (4.310 vs plain ctrl 4.666 — 0.36 nats from the auxiliary objective alone).
2. **The mask does not reliably buy reliance on its own.** worth_shuffle under
   mask+mux is seed-dependent (s1 fired, s2 did not).
3. **The curriculum composition is what buys ROBUST reliance.** gl1bc
   (warm-start → mask+mux) holds worth 0.05–0.097 at every eval while landing
   0.002 nats off gl1b-s1's CE. Its price against the mux'd unmasked ruler is
   ~0.09 nats.

So the design question for GL2 is no longer "how do we make the mask free" —
it is "is 0.09 nats a fair price for a guaranteed-load-bearing slot bottleneck"
(inference-time KV compression + the refiner loop say the bottleneck must
actually carry information to be worth looping on). Next falsifiers if the
line continues: gl1b seed 3 (breaks the reliance tie), gl1bc seed 2 (is the
curriculum's reliance robust across seeds?), and a param-matched nomask twin.

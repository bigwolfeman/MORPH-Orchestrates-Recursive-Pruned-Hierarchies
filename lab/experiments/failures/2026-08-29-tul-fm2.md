## Question

The plan is accurate (retrieval top-1 ~0.70) and unread (worth 0.0000; even the
true target planted at the prefix is worth 0.0000). Does direct read supervision —
the §5 emit CE at the slot position, whose one privileged information source is
`W_prefix(z)` — build the reading machinery that taxation (dropout), deletion
(CW), and rerouting (TG) never built?

## Hypothesis

Posterior-collapse framing (research synthesis, 2026-08-28): the read path never
had a gradient reason to exist. Emit CE supplies one. In every prior emit-on arm,
z was the unstable core loop's output; here z is accurate and stable. I expect the
reader to form at least partially — but the slot position ALSO sees full token
context through attention, and that shortcut has beaten the slot at every
checkpoint ever measured, so a null is a live possibility and would be decisive.

## The two findings

1. **Emit supervision helped CE and still built no reader.** `first_tok_ce`
   improved hugely (5.33 → 3.57) and overall CE beat FM1 by ~0.06 nats — but the
   counterfactual stayed negative throughout: the slot position got better at
   first tokens the same way every other position does, through the context
   shortcut. Direct read supervision at the slot was absorbed by the shortcut.
2. **K6 caught an interference channel I claimed could not exist.** Emit
   gradients cannot reach the planner's WEIGHTS (gated by test) — but they reach
   its TASK: they reshape the prelude features, which define the pooled targets
   y. Retrieval top-1 fell from 0.62 to 0.44 as emit pressure bent the target
   space toward what the context path finds convenient. The writer was degraded
   through its targets, not its parameters.

## Verdict

The pre-registered family-closing branch fires: tax (dropout), deletion (CW),
rerouting (TG), and now direct supervision (emit) are ALL null on the additive
prefix interface. The training-pressure family is exhausted. Next move per the
rule: interface surgery — which is exactly stage GL1 of
`.agents/notes/proposed/architecture/2026-08-29-gist-loop.md` (gisting mask +
gradient-carrying tap write, plans as first-class sequence participants),
approved in direction by Wolfe before this result landed. FM2b (span-BoW aux
head) remains available as a cheap bridge datum but is NOT required by the rule:
the myopic-target critique it addresses is now half-refuted anyway — emit
supervision was absorbed by the shortcut, not starved by myopia alone.

# Cell lens: what each slot-loop cell holds, per pass

Status: failure

## Question

What does each of the 4 cells hold, and when does it appear across loop passes? Two checkpoints,
both 5k on the fast recipe: the ungraded fan + pointer (`ab-nowta-ptr`, 4 independent lineages, coda
reads all 4) and LXTUL + pointer with the winner read (`ab-spdwin-r2`, cells reset to the router's
pick each pass, coda reads the winner). Wolfe 2026-10-10: "a future lens style decoding of each
cell".

## Hypothesis

The ungraded fan's cells are specialists: each holds the next span well for a subset of spans,
and on each span one cell is as good as all four (val oracle at 5k: cells alone 4.64-4.69, best
single cell 4.538, all four 4.537). LXTUL's cells are copies. In both, most content is present
after pass 1 (one pass does most of the work; K1-K6 is small).

## Predictions

Patch (frozen coda, coda-only CE of the next span, nats per token, the `fan/*` units):
- fan, depth 6: each cell alone 4.62-4.72; best single cell within 0.010 of all four; zero write
  at least 0.10 above all four; the most-winning cell takes 0.35-0.55 of spans.
- fan, depth 1 to depth 6: all-four CE falls by 0.000-0.015; the specialist pattern (best single
  within 0.010 of all four) holds at every depth.
- LXTUL, depth 6: the four cells alone within 0.04 of each other; the best single cell 0.01-0.07
  below the routed winner.
Probe (linear, cell -> tied embedding, next span tokens 1..4, val rows):
- unigram top-1 at offset 1 is 0.05-0.15; the best cell probe at offset 1 is at least 2x the
  unigram's top-1 (best guess top-1 0.25); offset 4 at most half of offset 1's gain over unigram.
- the shuffled-cell control is within 0.02 top-1 of unigram.
- fan: the all-cells-mean input beats every single cell; LXTUL: single cells and the mean within
  0.02 top-1 of each other.
- pass 0 (loop entry) to pass 6: probe CE at offset 1 falls by at most 0.05 nats.

## Verdict

Failure. Twelve of the 29 scored prediction lines failed or missed, among them the headline "the fan's cells are specialists"
(best single cell within 0.010 of all four at every depth, and a top cell share of 0.35-0.55) and
"the cross-span gap is mostly copying" (1.5x). The probe CE cannot be read, so its depth claim
is unscored.

The instrument worked. The patch, the copy split, the span decoder and the geometry all gave
clean, repeatable readings and the controls behaved (zero, shuffled and noise sit far below the
real cells). It found:

- The ungraded fan's cells are not strong specialists. The per-span best cell leads all four by
  0.012 at every depth, and cell 0 wins 0.59 of spans. Cell 0 also carries most of the copy worth (0.126 of 0.170).
- The novel-token signal is mostly in the combination: cells 0 and 2 alone are worse than zero on
  novel tokens, cells 1 and 3 give 0.019 and 0.024, all four give 0.123.
- One weak specialisation exists: cell 1 wins 61 % of its spans on whitespace-first spans.
- LXTUL cells differ at passes 1 and 2 (cell 2 reads 4.8769 at depth 1) and become copies from
  pass 3 (within-slot cos 0.83-0.88, CKA between passes 3 and 6 above 0.98).
- Both checkpoints decode about two tokens of the next span. From position 3 the accuracy is at
  the shuffled control's level.
- Copying is worth only 1.33x to 1.38x the novel worth per token, so copying does not explain
  most of the write's worth.

Not verified: the probe CE (method fault); any run-to-run spread (one run per part); whether the
cell 0 copy role is causal (patching only removes a cell, it does not move one).

## Updated hypothesis

The ungraded fan's cells split the work. Cell 0 carries cross-span copying. The novel-token signal
exists only in the combination of all four cells. One weak specialisation exists (cell 1, spans
that start with whitespace). LXTUL builds four strong cells that become copies from pass 3, and
picks one. Both hold the same amount of decodable next-span text, about two tokens. The fan's
0.013 coda-level lead comes from content that the coda reads and the span decoder does not.
Untested: cell 0 is a copy key for the coda's attention.

Next experiments:
1. A regularised probe (higher weight decay, early stop on held-out rows) so the probe CE is readable.
2. A copy-key test: patch cell 0 into another slot and measure copied-token CE on the source row's tokens.

## Addendum 2026-10-11: why LXTUL's cells become copies at pass 3

A code check, not a new run. Both checkpoints train the `epivol` diversity term on passes 1 and 2
only (`repel_passes=2` in the run banner). Under the latent-selected loop every continuing slot
enters pass t + 1 as four copies of pass t's winner (`_lsel_pass`, `reset_to_winner`), so from
pass 3 nothing pushes the four candidates apart and they stay near the winner (within-slot cos
0.83-0.88). The ungraded fan has the same term on the same passes, but its four lineages never
reset, so they stay apart after the term stops (cos -0.19 at pass 6). Consequence: LXTUL's
search breadth after pass 2 is close to one candidate.

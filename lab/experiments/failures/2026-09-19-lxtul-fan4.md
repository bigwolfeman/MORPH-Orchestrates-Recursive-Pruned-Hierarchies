# LXTUL fan4: do K latent streams through the shared core explore, or are they K copies?

Status: failure

Date: 2026-09-19. Commit: see the queue lines below. Arms: `slot-spandec-strict-fan4`
and its two controls, 5,000 steps, seq 1024, batch 6, seed 1, ramp 1,000, `norm_match`,
strict geometry, span decoder on. Written and committed BEFORE the arms ran on any GPU.
Nothing here has been fitted to a result: the only numbers measured so far are the CPU
wire check's, and they are labelled as such.

## Question

MORPH's TUL slot loop earns nothing from depth. Twelve arms read token K1−K6 inside
[−0.0001, +0.0033] against the plain looped model's +0.033, and pass 1 does 89–95 % of the
work. The 2026-09-18 latent-exploration survey
(`docs/references/looping-depth/latent-exploration/2026-09-18-latent-exploration-survey.md`)
lays nine papers on a 2x2 — {one stream, K streams} x {deterministic, stochastic} — and
finds three of the four cells already measured on this tree, all flat, and the fourth
empty. The empty cell is K streams present at TRAINING time WITH an explicit diversity
term, a learned selector, and an oracle-over-stream instrument.

The register (`tul.slot_cells: 4`, 2026-09-13) is the nearest thing we have run and it is
not that cell: four cells, no diversity term, no selector, no oracle. It collapsed —
`val/slot_cell_eff_rank` **1.2445** of 4 against a CPU init floor of 1.2383, within-slot
cosine **0.9432** against the fixture's 0.8443 (the cells ended the run CLOSER than they
began), token K1−K6 **+0.00197** against the ruler's +0.00161.

So the question this arm asks is not "does the register work". It is:

**Do a slot's K streams ever hold DIFFERENT usable continuations — at any pass, under a
term that pushes them apart and a gate that is allowed to pick — or is the best single
stream never better than the first one?**

The instrument that answers it is the oracle-over-stream: run the coda once per stream,
take the per-span minimum after the fact, and compare with stream 0. That is PLR's Figure
4 and Parallel-TTS's coverage@N in the units MORPH is scored in, and it is a WITHIN-MODEL
comparison — same weights, same rows, same tokens, same prefix width — so unlike every
cross-arm CE on this lineage it carries no packer and no width confound at all.

## Hypothesis

PLR (arXiv 2601.03153) Theorem 4.4 gives the closed form: under an L-Lipschitz shared
reasoning map, mean pairwise stream distance obeys `D(T) = L^(2T) D(0)`. MORPH's core is
measured contractive on the healthy arms (the slot-loop gain constraint holds the map at
0.89), so a contractive shared map and a demand for divergent streams are in direct
opposition and the register lost that argument. The hypothesis under test is that the
argument is winnable at the EARLY passes — where the collapse factor is `L^2` and not
`L^12` — and that a gate then has something to choose between.

The competing hypothesis, which the same literature supports, is that there is nothing to
explore over on this corpus: the Illusion paper's fine-tuned Coconut latents are worth
≤ 1.0 point, Single-threaded Reasoners shows the forward pass pruning a mixture to its
top-1 path in two or three layers, LTC measures between-thought variance falling 0.0244 →
0.0043 during training, and LTF's sampler, free to choose depth under an accuracy reward,
chose 1.9 steps. If that is right the oracle gap stays small however far apart the streams
are pushed, and the width branch of the exploration campaign closes.

Both hypotheses predict the repulsion CAN hold the streams apart geometrically. They
differ on whether that buys anything the coda can use. This is why the oracle and not the
cosine is the falsifier.

## Method

**Arms.** One config, three queue lines, one factor apart:

| name | overrides on `tul_slot_spandec_strict_fan4.yaml` | isolates |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `fan4` | none | the whole cell: K streams + repulsion + gate |
| `fan4-norepel` | `tul.fan_repel_lambda=0` | the gate alone (PLR's own ablation order) |
| `fan4-mean` | `tul.fan_mix=mean tul.fan_repel_lambda=0` | K streams alone |

The standing partner is `slot-spandec-strict` at **`tul.prefix_k=4`**, which is the exact
width and packer `fan4` runs at: the fan mixes its four streams to ONE state and writes it
through the ordinary single-source `prefix_project`, so its coda input has the shape a
`prefix_k: 4` ruler's has. Cross-arm CE against the k=2 ruler carries the packer confound
the register record names and is context only.

**Reading order.** (1) `fan/oracle_ce` vs `fan/single_ce` — P-1, and it decides. (2) the
per-pass `fan/stream_cos_t{t}` and `fan/stream_rank_t{t}`, NEVER their sum
(`depth-summing-instruments-hide-pass-trades`). (3) the K-curve. (4) `fan/mixed_ce`,
`val/slot_eff_rank`, `worth_profile`, as context.

**Probes at 2,500 and 5,000:** `core_depth_sweep.py --depths 1,2,3,6,9,12,16 --rows 480`
(the K-curve), `span_budget_profile.py --config budget_web_full --depths 6` (the paired
depth-6 gap against the prefix_k 4 partner), the shipped `[VAL]` slot-state probe
(`val/slot_cell_eff_rank`, `val/slot_cell_pairwise_cos`, `val/slot_eff_rank`), and
`worth_profile` zero / shuffle / wrong_seed / all_slots.

**Amendments.** The Predictions section above is frozen. If the method has to change, it
is amended here with the date and the reason, per `lab/experiments/README.md`; if the
change makes the run unable to answer the question, the run is rejected and a new planned
file is opened.

**What has actually been run so far, and it is not a result.** A CPU wire check at
`data.seq_len=256`, batch 1, `CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=""`, on the real 297.7M-parameter
config: build 0.9 s, train forward 6.5 s, backward 7.7 s, loss 22.66 finite, eval forward
9.7 s including the four oracle coda passes, every `fan/*` key finite. At INIT the
collapse signature is already the theorem's shape — `fan/stream_cos_t0..t6` 0.690, 0.745,
0.804, 0.848, 0.879, 0.901, 0.917 and `fan/stream_rank_t0..t6` 1.373 → 1.407 of 4 — but
this is a random model on 42 synthetic spans and it predicts nothing about a trained one.

## Verdict

Failure. The falsifier is met on its letter by a constant offset the clause did not
anticipate, and on its meaning (choice over the streams, 0.008) it fails; P-3, P-4 and
P-5 fail; the fan is 0.0073 nats worse than its exact-width ruler at depth 6. The
repulsion produced a rank-1 sign split on a fixed axis, which the two follow-up arms
replaced (epiplexity: gamed by a one-hot stream; volume plus epiplexity: not gamed, and
the oracle still reads 0.018). The K-stream branch is closed by the three arms together.

## Updated hypothesis

The number that decides a K-stream arm is `oracle − mixed` with `stream_ce_k{i} − mixed`
beside it, never `single − oracle` (a single stream is off-mixture whenever any term acts
on the streams). On this reader and corpus that number is 0.008 to 0.018 with or without a
diversity term, and the width partner beats every fan arm on paired rows. Nothing at the
span level for the loop to explore over; the width of the prefix is worth more than
four streams of it (pk4 − mean −0.0078).

# Flat K/V kernels (K1) alone on the speed base at 5k

Status: failure

## Question

The K1 + P0 5k check failed, and the split put the failure on P0
(`failures/2026-10-13-k1-p0-5k.md`): K1 alone was clean to step 600 on two seeds. K1
(`model.kv_flat_kernels`) is rounding only (sum order) and saves 3.9 ms on the pinned bench. Does
the speed base with K1 alone train the same function at 5k?

## Hypothesis

K1 changes rounding only. The arm sits inside the seed spread of the G1 runs.

## Verdict

Failure (inconclusive, a protocol failure). CE@6 and stability hold, but the K1-K6 mean is 87.7 %
of the control at n = 3, under the 95 % rule. The paired spread (one seed differs by -0.0012)
is larger than the 5 % margin the rule tests (0.00027), so more seeds of this design cannot
resolve it cheaply. K1 stays OFF.

## Updated hypothesis

K1 changes only the sum order of weight-grad partials; there is no measured mechanism for a loop
effect, and the paired mean difference is -0.0003 +- 0.0005. The method cannot tell a 5 % loop
change from zero: the per-seed K1-K6 standard deviation (about 0.0015) is 25 % of its mean, so a
5 % test at this power needs on the order of 30+ seed pairs. Next options, Wolfe's call: (a) seeds 4
and 5 of both arms (narrows the error to about 0.0004, still not 5 %); (b) accept K1 under a
paired-difference rule for rounding-only changes; (c) leave K1 off (3.9 ms per step).

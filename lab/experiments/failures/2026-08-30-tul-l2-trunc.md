# Planned: L2-TRUNC — does the cap's depth-earning loop survive BPTT truncation?

Status: failure
Date: 2026-08-30 (frozen before launch). Config: `tul_l2trunc.yaml` (= tul_l2 +

## Question

tul-l2-cap earns 0.233 nats from its loop (depth sweep 4.6220 @1 → 4.3892 @6) under
FULL BPTT. The production/base recipe truncates BPTT at 4 of up to 8 iterations. Does
the cap's win survive when only the last 4 iterations carry gradient?

depth curve 0.233 nats; σ pinned 1.50. Replicate CE spread 0.030–0.036.

## Verdict

Failure — the two load-bearing predictions (PT2, PT3) landed opposite my priors. The
method distinguished cleanly; the answer is just "no": the depth-earning loop is a
full-BPTT phenomenon. Recipe consequence recorded above.

## Updated hypothesis

Iterations that carry no gradient cannot learn to carry computation, even when the
forward runs them and the cap keeps them well-conditioned — the loop's value lives
in the trained coupling across ALL iterations, not in the last-k window. Any
production schedule that wants the 0.233-nat loop must budget full BPTT on the slot
sequence.

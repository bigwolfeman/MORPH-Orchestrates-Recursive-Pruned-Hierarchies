# Planned: ARC E12 — the k=12 panel: four TUL variants at a fixed slot depth of 12

Status: planned
Date: 2026-09-07 (frozen before the smoke; Wolfe: "we need to do a fixed run at k of 12",
all four variants)
Arc: `2026-09-04-loop-contribution-arc.md`.

## Question

Every stable slot loop so far was trained at a Poisson mean of 6 and does its work by
iteration 3 (half its mean; the normal shape for a looped model, Wolfe 2026-09-07). E6
showed that a loop trained at a deeper draw depends on its depth (K3−K6 0.277 at mean 16)
and E7 showed the slot loop at mean 16 detonates under the hinge alone. With the
fixed-point term now on the slot loop and the depth FIXED (no draw: every slot loops
exactly 12 times, train and eval, so the gradient window is uniform), what does each TUL
variant do with 12 iterations, what does it cost, and do the tokens read it?

## Method

Four arms, one seed each, 5000 steps, the panel recipe (ramp 1000, flat 1e-4, batch 6,
seq 1024, hinge 0.9 with clip 4.0, `core_fixed_point_lambda 1.0`), sustained tripwire,
`tul.slot_depth_fixed 12`, `slot_max_depth 12`, `bptt_depth 12` (full BPTT):

| arm | config | what it is |
|---|---|---|
| mask | `tul_k12_mnext_mask.yaml` | E4's arm: forecast MUX, tokens see the span only through the slot |
| mnext | `tul_k12_mnext.yaml` | Y2: forecast MUX, tokens keep the direct route |
| mtp | `tul_k12_mnext_mask_mtp4.yaml` | mask + 4 lookahead heads on the TUL coda (parallel decode) |
| a1 | `tul_k12_a1.yaml` | A1 classic: bag-mean seed, emit/plast 0.5/0.5, no MUX, no mask |

Readouts at 2500 and 5000: `core_depth_sweep.py` at forced slot depths 1, 2, 3, 6, 9, 12,
16 on the arc's 480 rows (token CE, span-first CE, `mux_local` where built, paired
bootstraps over rows); `worth_profile.py` at 5000 (192 rows); the `train/fixed_point` and
`preclip/total` traces; wall clock from the queue-log epochs. Rulers: E4 at 5000
(`results/2026-09-07-arc-e4/sweep_to-mnext-y2-mask_5000.json`, token CE 4.336 at depth 6),
Y2 at 5000 (4.205), A3 at 5000 (4.0208, the cheap-decode floor), E8-6 on notul (+0.345
next-token CE from the heads at weight 1.0). Order: mask, mnext, a1, mtp. Runner
`arc/run_e12.sh`. Cost: E4 ran 68 min at mean 6 (eager); at fixed 12 the masked arms are
~2 h each, the unmasked ~80 min: about 7 h with readouts.

## Predictions (frozen)

- **P12a.** All four arms reach 5000 with the sustained tripwire silent: **45 %** (each
  ~80 %; E7 at mean 16 detonated at 2712 under the hinge alone).
- **P12b (depth dependence).** On the mask arm at 5000, forecast `mux_local` K6−K12 > 0.01
  with the paired CI above 0: **50 %**; K3−K6 > 0.01: **60 %** (E6: the draw is the lever).
- **P12c (the tokens).** On the mask arm, token K6−K12 ≥ 0.005: **35 %** (E4 read the
  slot's depth at 0.021 for K1−K6 on mean 6; every unmasked arm ≤ 0.0006).
- **P12d (value, the E6 lesson).** The mask arm at depth 12 beats E4 (mean 6) at depth 6
  on the 480 rows at 5000, paired CE lower with CI below 0: **30 %** (E6's deep model was
  0.104 WORSE at matched steps).
- **P12e (the unmasked arm).** mnext's token K1−K12 stays ≤ 0.002 (the direct route
  wins whatever the slot computes): **65 %**.
- **P12f (parallel decode).** The mtp arm's next-token CE at depth 12 is within 0.35 of
  the mask arm's (E8's penalty on notul), and its head-2 CE beats the mask arm's token
  CE at depth 12 by less than 0.5: **50 %** / **60 %**.
- **P12g (A1).** a1 reaches 5000 and its token CE at depth 12 is within 0.10 of mnext's:
  **45 %** (A1's disease was the takeover; the hinge and the term are new to it).
- **P12h (cost).** The mask arm's wall clock is ≤ 2.2x E4's 68 min: **60 %**.

## Binding

- P12b TRUE and P12d TRUE ⇒ the k=12 mask arm THINKS and pays: it is the arm; the 20k
  matched-wall-clock verdict against A3-20k is Wolfe's call, not queued.
- P12b TRUE and P12d FALSE ⇒ dependence without value again (E6 on the slot loop): the
  lever is not depth but what the slot is asked to compute; E3 (staged targets) next, at
  k=12.
- P12b FALSE ⇒ a fixed 12-iteration slot loop still finishes by 3–6: the target is the
  binding problem on the slot path; E3 at k=12 and the M-own target at k=12 are next.
- P12a FALSE on any arm ⇒ that arm's trip step, `preclip/*` and `train/fixed_point`
  shape are filed; the fixed-point term did not hold the slot loop at 12 and the
  divergence README's regime table gets the row.

# Planned: raise span_cap 32 → 64, so a slot summarises a thought and not a budget

Status: planned
Config: `morph/configs/tul_tg2c64.yaml`
Measurement behind it: `/home/wolfe/morph-scratch/smoke/span_cap_capfrac.py`, 1.9 M real OWT tokens

## The defect

At the shipped `span_cap: 32`, **26.9% of spans are ended by the BUDGET, not by a real
boundary.** A span cut at the cap is not a semantic unit, and the TUL thesis is that a slot
summarises one. More than a quarter of our "thoughts" are arbitrary truncations.

This was already in the tree and not treated as a problem: `base.yaml`'s `gate_k_max`
comment records "Measured on OWT at span_cap 32, 24.5% of labels are a span of exactly 32"
— as a reason to give the GATE headroom, never as a reason to question the cap. Two
independent measurements, 24.5% and 26.9%, agree that the cap binds on about a quarter of
spans.

## Why this lever and not `max_slots`

`span_cap` is FREE where `max_slots` is not. The core loops a fixed `max_slots`-wide compact
sequence (`_tul_core` → `gather_valid`, a MASKED update, never a per-position gather), so
longer spans mean FEWER spans per row at the SAME core width: **+2.00% token positions,
+0.0% core compute, KV compression 9.85× → 12.47×.** Arm A4c (`max_slots` 64 → 80) buys
+0.91% tokens for **+25% core compute**.

## Why the old objection no longer stands

The pooling law — slot-seed signal falling 0.516 (spans 4-5) to 0.210 (spans 24-32) against
a constant ‖E_slot‖ 0.238 — was the ONLY argument that ever favoured a short cap. Arm TG4a
deleted the bag-mean seed outright and moved ce_main by **0.003 nats** (4.791 vs TG2's
4.794, inside the 0.036 seed spread). If deleting the seed entirely costs nothing, diluting
it cannot cost much either. Thought Gestalt's own ablation runs the same way: max sentence
length 64 beats 32 by 0.6 PPL.

Chose 64 over 56 (nearer the 24.32 target): AT-CAP halves again, 7.0% → 4.5%, for +0.14%
tokens. Past 64 the curve is flat.

## Predictions (frozen 2026-08-28 03:30, before this arm has run one step)

Metric **ce_main** at step 3000 from `slot_path_worth.py`. Read against TG2's own seeds —
4.8763 / 4.7121, mean **4.794** — which differ from this arm by the cap alone. Control band
4.4586–4.5459 for orientation only.

**C1 (it does not diverge).** 0 of 2 seeds fire the takeover rule. This arm is tul_tg2-based
and every tul_tg2-based seed has held (0 of 4). Longer spans mean fewer, longer-lived slot
states, which is not obviously neutral for the core map — so this is a real prediction, not
a formality.

**C2 (CE improves).** Mean ce_main@3000 over the two seeds is BELOW TG2's 4.794. Predicted
on the MEAN: within-arm seed spread this campaign is 0.036–0.16 nats, so seed-matched n=1
comparison is not readable.

**C3 (the gain is small).** The improvement is under 0.10 nats. +2.00% token positions and
6× fewer truncated spans is a data-quality change, not an architectural one, and nothing
this campaign has tried has moved ce_main by more than ~0.05 outside of divergence.

**C4 (the loop still misses its line).** Neither seed reaches loop worth ≥ 0.05 on ce_main
at either checkpoint. This arm is `bag_mean`, so the plain `no-loop` column is meaningful
here — unlike on TG4a/TG4b.

## Decision rule

- **C2 HOLDS and the gain exceeds 0.10 (C3 fails)** → span quality, not architecture, was a
  live share of the deficit. Re-run the CONTROL at cap 64 next, because the same free change
  would then apply to the whole recipe and the band itself should move.
- **C2 HOLDS under 0.10** → adopt cap 64 as the default (it is free and strictly better on
  AT-CAP, tokens and KV) but do NOT count it as progress on the deficit.
- **C2 FAILS** → span truncation was not costing us anything measurable, the AT-CAP defect
  is cosmetic at this scale, and the packing levers are closed. Combined with the seed
  result and the mask result, that closes the TG track's CE story and leaves A7
  (FlexAttention) as the only item worth GPU time.
- **C1 FAILS** → longer spans are takeover-relevant; that outranks the CE question.

## Method

Two seeds (1, 2), 3500 steps, batch 6, `use_kernels=false`, `ademamix_alpha_cap=3.5`,
`grad_probe_every=1`, ckpt every 500 — identical to every arm in this campaign. Sequential
on the 5090 (UPS). Worth passes at 3000 and 3500. Takeover rule unchanged.

`gate_k_max` is raised 40 → 80 to keep base.yaml's stated 1.25 × span_cap rule. The gate is
OFF here so the check never runs, but leaving 40 would make the config raise the moment
anyone set `gate: true` — the "config key that silently means something else" trap this tree
fails loudly on elsewhere.

NOT controlled, and named: this changes the DATA, not only the packing. Rows carry 2% more
token positions and spans are 27% longer, so the arm is not token-matched to TG2. That is
inherent to the lever — holding tokens fixed would mean shortening the row, which
re-introduces the truncation being removed.

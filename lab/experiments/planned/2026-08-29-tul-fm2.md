## Question

The plan is accurate (retrieval top-1 ~0.70) and unread (worth 0.0000; even the
true target planted at the prefix is worth 0.0000). Does direct read supervision —
the §5 emit CE at the slot position, whose one privileged information source is
`W_prefix(z)` — build the reading machinery that taxation (dropout), deletion
(CW), and rerouting (TG) never built?

## Hypothesis

Posterior-collapse framing (research synthesis, 2026-08-28): the read path never
had a gradient reason to exist. Emit CE supplies one. In every prior emit-on arm,
z was the unstable core loop's output; here z is accurate and stable. I expect the
reader to form at least partially — but the slot position ALSO sees full token
context through attention, and that shortcut has beaten the slot at every
checkpoint ever measured, so a null is a live possibility and would be decisive.

## Question

When the coda cannot see tokens before position 576 (only the last 448 tokens plus
every slot), does the plan become worth nats — or is the prefix pathway itself
unable to deliver plan content to the CE?

## Hypothesis

The plan pays once it is the only carrier. The CW eval screen measured +0.029 nats
(CW1 vs CW3) for slots that were never trained under forced reliance; a co-trained,
70%-retrieval-accurate plan under a hard cut should be worth well more than the
0.01 gate. If it is still zero, the fault is the W_prefix/coda pathway or the plan's
content basis, not the planner.

## Predictions (frozen — not edited after launch)

- **G1 stability.** Run completes 4500 steps. No sustained detonation:
  `val/ce_tokens` never sits more than 0.20 nats above its running minimum for two
  or more CONSECUTIVE evals after step 1000. (FM1's 0.10 single-eval threshold was
  miscalibrated — a lone 0.15 bounce that recovered tripped it.) Predict HOLDS.
- **G2 speed.** ≥ 3.3 steps/s — the gathered coda runs 576 positions instead of
  1152 (FM1 measured 3.30 at full length). Predict 3.4–4.2.
- **G3 THE GATE — plan worth.** `val/plan_worth_shuffle` ≥ 0.01 nats sustained
  (≥3 consecutive evals) by step 3000. **Predict it FIRES** — point range at 4250:
  worth_shuffle in [0.02, 0.15], worth_zero in [0.05, 0.5] (zeroing removes ALL
  pre-cut content; shuffling swaps plans between slots of the same row).
- **G4 retrieval survives.** `val/copy_gap` stays > 0.2 at every eval ≥ 1000.
- **G5 target health.** `val/target_eff_rank` ≥ 100 and `val/target_pairwise_cos`
  < 0.1 by step 4500 (SIGReg replicated from FM1).
- **Secondary, no gate.** `val/ce_tokens` here is CE over tokens ≥ 576 ONLY —
  not comparable to A3/FM1. Sanity band @4250: [4.2, 5.8]. Outside that band is a
  finding to explain, not a scored prediction.

## Verdict rule

- SUCCESS = G1 & G3. First paying plan in the program → seed 2, then a cut sweep
  (harder cuts toward Wolfe's literal "last 2 spans").
- G3 silent (worth ≈ 0 with the channel deleted) is the decisive negative: the
  planner is exonerated twice over, and the next probe is the W_prefix/coda
  pathway (can the coda decode ANY vector planted at a slot prefix?) — not more
  training. Files to `failures/` with that probe as the named next step.
- G1 failing under CW is new information about the gather path, filed as its own
  finding.

## Method

Identical to FM1's launch (`failures/2026-08-28-tul-fm1-live-arm.md` Method) except
`--config-name tul_fm1_cw` and `wandb.name=fm1-cw-s1`:
steps 4500, batch 6, seed 1, alpha_cap 3.5, t_beta3=3500, use_kernels=false,
eval_every 250, gen_every 0, ckpt_every 500, grad_probe_every 1,
`fm.source_std=0.03125`. One trainer on the GPU (UPS rule).

Ships with one code fix (same commit): the CW training gather now masks slot emit
labels to −100, matching the eval screen's scoring contract — unpatched, CW
training would have silently reinstated the emit loss at weight 1.0. Gated by
`test_cw_scores_only_the_kept_token_positions` and the rewritten
`test_forward_coda_token_cut_positive_drops_only_early_tokens`.

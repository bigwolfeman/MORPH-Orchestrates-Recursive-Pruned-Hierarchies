# Cell lens: what each slot-loop cell holds, per pass

Status: planned

## Question

What does each of the 4 cells hold, and when does it appear across loop passes? Two checkpoints,
both 5k on the fast recipe: the ungraded fan + pointer (`ab-nowta-ptr`, 4 independent lineages, coda
reads all 4) and LXTUL + pointer with the winner read (`ab-spdwin-r2`, cells reset to the router's
pick each pass, coda reads the winner). Wolfe 2026-10-10: "a future lens style decoding of each
cell".

## Hypothesis

The ungraded fan's cells are specialists: each holds the next span well for a subset of spans,
and on each span one cell is as good as all four (val oracle at 5k: cells alone 4.64-4.69, best
single cell 4.538, all four 4.537). LXTUL's cells are copies. In both, most content is present
after pass 1 (one pass does most of the work; K1-K6 is small).

## Predictions

Patch (frozen coda, coda-only CE of the next span, nats per token, the `fan/*` units):
- fan, depth 6: each cell alone 4.62-4.72; best single cell within 0.010 of all four; zero write
  at least 0.10 above all four; the most-winning cell takes 0.35-0.55 of spans.
- fan, depth 1 to depth 6: all-four CE falls by 0.000-0.015; the specialist pattern (best single
  within 0.010 of all four) holds at every depth.
- LXTUL, depth 6: the four cells alone within 0.04 of each other; the best single cell 0.01-0.07
  below the routed winner.
Probe (linear, cell -> tied embedding, next span tokens 1..4, val rows):
- unigram top-1 at offset 1 is 0.05-0.15; the best cell probe at offset 1 is at least 2x the
  unigram's top-1 (best guess top-1 0.25); offset 4 at most half of offset 1's gain over unigram.
- the shuffled-cell control is within 0.02 top-1 of unigram.
- fan: the all-cells-mean input beats every single cell; LXTUL: single cells and the mean within
  0.02 top-1 of each other.
- pass 0 (loop entry) to pass 6: probe CE at offset 1 falls by at most 0.05 nats.

## Method

`lab/divergence/cell_lens.py` (commit adding this file): `--part data --config nowta_pointer_fast
--train-skip 5000 --train-batches 64 --val-batches 40` (val rows skip 50,000 documents), then
`--part patch --depths 1,2,3,6 --n-val 40` and `--part probe --depths 1,2,3,6 --offsets 4 --steps
3000 --n-train 64 --n-val 40` on each checkpoint, under `gpu.lock`, between training runs.
Tests: `tests/test_cell_lens.py` 11 passed CPU; sabotage of the stream write fails 5.

## Amendment 2026-10-11: four added parts (written before any of them ran)

Added to `cell_lens.py` after the fan patch above ran: a copied / novel split of patch and probe,
`--part special` (which span properties the per-span best cell tracks), `--part spandec` (the
model's own span decoder, free-running, per cell), `--part geom` (CKA and effective rank per
pass). Tests `tests/test_cell_lens_ext.py` 17 passed CPU; sabotage of the copied rule
(`seen < start` to `<=`) fails 5. "Copied" = the target token appears at a text position of the
row before its span starts (cross-span copy; within-span repeats do not count).

Predictions (depth 6 unless stated; the pointer head is off in every part):
- Copy split, patch: copied tokens are 0.45-0.60 of scored tokens. The write's worth (zero minus
  all four) per copied token is at least 1.5x its worth per novel token, on both checkpoints
  (memory: the cross-span gap is mostly copying).
- Special, fan: NMI(best cell; property) sits above the permutation null's p95 for at least one
  of length, copy ratio, difficulty, first-token class; the largest NMI is 0.005-0.05 (cells
  specialise weakly). LX: no property above the null's p95 by more than 0.005 NMI.
- Spandec (free-running, greedy, true length): fan, the cell mean (its trained input) top-1 at
  position 1 is 0.15-0.35; every single cell at least 0.02 below the mean. LX (trained input =
  the winner): the four single cells within 0.02 of each other. Both: the RMS-noise and
  other-slot controls at least 0.05 below the trained input at position 1; position-8 accuracy
  at most half of position 1; exact match at most 0.03.
- Geom (diagnostic only, no verdict rides on it): fan between-cell CKA at pass 6 below 0.5, LX
  above 0.8; pass 1 vs pass 6 CKA per cell above 0.8 on both; pooled effective rank of the fan
  at least 1.5x LX's.

Method: a second chain (`/home/wolfe/morph-scratch/abq/lens/chain2.sh`, a fresh tree copy at the
commit adding these parts) waits for the first chain's ALLDONE, then runs patch on both
checkpoints (new output files, so the copy split and the per-span columns exist), special on
both patch files (CPU), spandec on both, and geom on both together, all under `gpu.lock`, on the
same `batches.pt` (both configs pack prefix_k 4).

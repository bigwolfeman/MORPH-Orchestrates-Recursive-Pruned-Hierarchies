# Flat K/V kernels (K1) alone on the speed base at 5k

Status: planned

## Question

The K1 + P0 5k check failed, and the split put the failure on P0
(`failures/2026-10-13-k1-p0-5k.md`): K1 alone was clean to step 600 on two seeds. K1
(`model.kv_flat_kernels`) is rounding only (sum order) and saves 3.9 ms on the pinned bench. Does
the speed base with K1 alone train the same function at 5k?

## Hypothesis

K1 changes rounding only. The arm sits inside the seed spread of the G1 runs.

## Predictions

New arm: `nowta_pointer_fast_spdcells_speed_bf16c_cellp3` + `model.kv_flat_kernels=true`,
seeds 1 and 2. Controls: the filed S5 + G1 runs, CE@6 3.9023 / 3.9033, K1-K6 +0.0057 / +0.0067
(means 3.9028 / +0.0062; `successes/2026-10-13-g1-triton-gemm-5k.md`).

| Arm | Seed | CE@6 best guess | K1-K6 best guess |
|---|---|---|---|
| K1 on the speed base | 1 | 3.904 | +0.0060 |
| K1 on the speed base | 2 | 3.904 | +0.0060 |

- Decision: K1 goes ON in `nowta_pointer_fast_spdcells_speed_bf16c` if the arm's mean CE@6 minus
  3.9028 is <= +0.003 AND its mean K1-K6 is >= +0.00589 (95 % of +0.0062). If CE@6 holds and
  K1-K6 sits in [+0.0039, +0.00589): "not resolved at n=2", a third seed of the arm and of the
  control. Below +0.0039: K1 stays off.
- The cell knob survives: gap(1) mean <= +0.015 and gap(2) mean <= +0.003.
- Stability on both runs: grad-norm max/median <= 30, `tul/gain_est` inside 0.85-0.95 from step
  1000 to 5000, and no step after 60 with gain above 0.95.

## Method

The same tree as the failed check, `abq/tree-kvf` (master 2c8a1216, K1 code unchanged since).
`abq/chain-k1.sh`: seeds 1 and 2, 5000 steps each, then `core_depth_sweep.py --depths 1,6

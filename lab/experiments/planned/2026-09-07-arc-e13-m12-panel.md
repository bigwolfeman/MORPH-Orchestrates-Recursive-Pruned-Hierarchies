# Planned: ARC E13 — the mean-12 panel: four TUL variants at a Poisson slot draw of mean 12

Status: planned
Date: 2026-09-07 (frozen before the smoke; Wolfe: "MEAN DEPTH 12. Use Poisson!", one run
per variant, all four variants)
Arc: `2026-09-04-loop-contribution-arc.md`. Replaces E12
(`failures/2026-09-07-arc-e12-k12-panel.md`), which ran a FIXED depth by my misreading.

## Question

Every stable slot loop so far was trained at a Poisson mean of 6 and does its work by
iteration 3 (half its mean; the normal shape for a looped model). E6 showed that a plain
loop trained at a deeper draw depends on its depth (K3−K6 0.277 at mean 16), E7 showed the
mask arm at mean 16 (max 24, grad through 8) detonates under the hinge alone, and E12's
one run showed the mask arm at a FIXED depth of 12 under full BPTT enters E7's
power-iteration mode by step 900 (first-iteration state scale x170, hinge blind).
With the fixed-point term on the slot loop and the per-slot draw at Poisson mean 12 (max
16, full BPTT so no sample loses its gradient window), what does each TUL variant do with
a mean of 12 iterations, does it survive, what does it cost, and do the tokens read it?

## Method

Four arms, one seed each, 5000 steps, the panel recipe (ramp 1000, flat 1e-4, batch 6,
seq 1024, hinge 0.9 with clip 4.0, `core_fixed_point_lambda 1.0`), sustained tripwire,
`tul.slot_mean_depth 12`, `slot_max_depth 16`, `bptt_depth 16` (full BPTT: no no-grad
prefix, so the gradient-window bug note does not apply):

| arm | config | what it is |
|---|---|---|
| mask | `tul_m12_mnext_mask.yaml` | E4's arm: forecast MUX, tokens see the span only through the slot |
| mnext | `tul_m12_mnext.yaml` | Y2: forecast MUX, tokens keep the direct route |
| a1 | `tul_m12_a1.yaml` | A1 classic: bag-mean seed, emit/plast 0.5/0.5, no MUX, no mask |
| mtp | `tul_m12_mnext_mask_mtp4.yaml` | mask + 4 lookahead heads on the TUL coda (parallel decode) |

Readouts at 2500 and 5000: `core_depth_sweep.py` at forced slot depths 1, 2, 3, 6, 9, 12,
16 on the arc's 480 rows (token CE, span-first CE, `mux_local` where built, paired
bootstraps over rows); `worth_profile.py` at 5000 (192 rows); the `train/fixed_point`,
`loop/core_gain_t0` and `preclip/total` traces; wall clock from the queue-log epochs.
Rulers: E4 at 5000 (`results/2026-09-07-arc-e4/sweep_to-mnext-y2-mask_5000.json`, token CE
4.336 at depth 6), Y2 at 5000 (4.205), A3 at 5000 (4.0208, the cheap-decode floor), E8-6
on notul (+0.345 next-token CE from the heads at weight 1.0), E7's mask arm at mean 16
(detonated 2712; its 2500 checkpoint is pre-onset and not citable). Order: mask, mnext,
a1, mtp. Runner `arc/run_e13.sh`. Cost: E4 ran 68 min at mean 6 (eager); at mean 12 the
masked arms are ~2 h each, the unmasked ~80 min: about 7 h with readouts.

A 2500 checkpoint of an arm that later detonates is pre-onset and is NOT cited for depth
earning (the arc's rule since E2).

## Predictions (frozen)

- **P13a.** All four arms reach 5000 with the sustained tripwire silent: **15 %** (each
  ~45 %; E7 at mean 16 detonated at 2712, E12's fixed-12 mask arm at 1446; the term and
  the hinge are both scale-blind to the E7 mode).
- **P13b (depth dependence).** On the mask arm at its LAST checkpoint that is not
  pre-onset, forecast `mux_local` K6−K12 > 0.01 with the paired CI above 0: **50 %**;
  K3−K6 > 0.01: **60 %** (E6: the draw is the lever; E7's pre-onset 2500 cleared both).
- **P13c (the tokens).** On the mask arm, token K6−K12 ≥ 0.005: **35 %** (E4 read the
  slot's depth at 0.021 for K1−K6 on mean 6; every unmasked arm ≤ 0.0006).
- **P13d (value, the E6 lesson).** The mask arm at forced depth 12 beats E4 (mean 6) at
  depth 6 on the 480 rows at 5000, paired CE lower with CI below 0: **25 %** (E6's deep
  model was 0.104 WORSE at matched steps; scored only if the arm reaches 5000).
- **P13e (the unmasked arm).** mnext's token K1−K12 stays ≤ 0.002 (the direct route
  wins whatever the slot computes): **65 %**.
- **P13f (parallel decode).** The mtp arm's next-token CE at forced depth 12 is within
  0.35 of the mask arm's (E8's penalty on notul), and its head-2 CE beats the mask arm's
  token CE at depth 12 by less than 0.5: **50 %** / **60 %** (scored at matched
  checkpoints).
- **P13g (A1).** a1 reaches 5000 and its token CE at depth 12 is within 0.10 of mnext's:
  **40 %** (A1's disease was the takeover; the hinge and the term are new to it).
- **P13h (cost).** The mask arm's wall clock is ≤ 2.2x E4's 68 min, or its projected
  5000-step wall clock from the queue-log epochs if it trips: **60 %**.
- **P13i (the E7 mode).** On any arm that trips, `loop/core_gain_t0` exceeds 10 before
  `preclip/total` first exceeds 1e3 (the state's first-iteration scale leads the spike
  train, as in E12 and E7): **75 %**.

## Binding

- P13b TRUE and P13d TRUE ⇒ the mean-12 mask arm THINKS and pays: it is the arm; the 20k
  matched-wall-clock verdict against A3-20k is Wolfe's call, not queued.
- P13b TRUE and P13d FALSE ⇒ dependence without value again (E6 on the slot loop): the
  lever is not depth but what the slot is asked to compute; E3 (staged targets) next, at
  mean 12.
- P13b FALSE ⇒ a mean-12 slot loop still finishes by 3–6: the target is the binding
  problem on the slot path; E3 at mean 12 and the M-own target at mean 12 are next.
- P13a FALSE on any arm ⇒ that arm's trip step, `loop/core_gain_t0`, `preclip/*` and
  `train/fixed_point` shape are filed and the divergence README's regime table gets the
  row. If P13i holds, the lever that bounds the STATE (`model.slot_state_renorm`, the
  other measured stability lever, 0 spikes to 5000 at mean 6) is the next arm at mean 12;
  Wolfe's call, not queued.

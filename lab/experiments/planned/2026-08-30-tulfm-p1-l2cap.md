## Question

P1's retrieval gates failed twice on tul_a3 states (within-row top-1 0.0235 then
0.0423 vs the ≥0.06 bar) — states whose plans we have since measured as first-token
spikes at best. tul-l2-cap's slot states are span-wide carriers (worth_profile:
shuffle +0.22 at offset 0, +0.11 at offset 16+; worth 0.146). Does a planner trained
on THESE states pass the same frozen gates?

## Reference numbers (fixed)

P1 on a3: R2 within-row top-1 0.0235 / 0.0423 (two runs), bar ≥0.06, MRR bar ≥0.12.
Untrained floor and shuffled_ctx controls per docs/tul-fm-probing.md §5. Chance:
batch-wide ≈0.0023, within-row ≈0.0187.

## Predictions (frozen)

- **RV1.** Same gates, same pipeline (train_p1 4000 steps, retrieval_probe, both
  controls): within-row top-1 ≥ 0.06 AND MRR ≥ 0.12 on l2cap states: 40%. My prior:
  the substrate is measurably informative now, but the planner must model the NEXT
  span's latent from context — reading the state ≠ predicting the successor.
- **RV2.** Within-row top-1 at least 2x the best a3 reading (≥ 0.085): 25%.
- **RV3.** Controls behave (untrained ≈ floor, shuffled_ctx drops toward chance): 85%.
- **Decision rule (binding).** RV1 passes ⇒ FM earns a revival prereg for a live arm
  ON the l2cap recipe, with the FM2 grad-share guard mandatory from step 0. RV1 fails
  with RV3 clean ⇒ the FM planner line stays DEAD — even the best-measured substrate
  is not enough, and the next lever is the objective, not the states.

## Method

1. `python -m lab.tulfm.train_p1 backbone.config_name=tul_l2
   backbone.checkpoint=checkpoints/morph/tul-l2-cap/step_4500.pt
   run.ckpt_dir=checkpoints/tulfm/p1-l2cap wandb.name=tulfm-p1-l2cap` (other keys
   untouched from tulfm_p1.yaml — same steps/lr/batch as the a3 runs).
2. `python -m lab.tulfm.retrieval_probe --planner checkpoints/tulfm/p1-l2cap/step_4000.pt
   --out lab/experiments/results/2026-08-30-tulfm-p1-l2cap/probe.json` (probe composes
   the backbone from the planner ckpt's logged config).
3. Score against the gates verbatim; file with artifacts.

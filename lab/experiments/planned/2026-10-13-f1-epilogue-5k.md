# SwiGLU epilogue fusion (F1) on the speed base at 5k

Status: planned

## Question

`model.gemm_epilogue_fusion` (F1, merge 10cb1abd) moves the SwiGLU forward into the `gate_up`
GEMM's epilogue and the SwiGLU backward into the `down` dgrad epilogue. It is rounding only: the
fixed-weight readout did not move (CE@6 3.9060, K1-K6 +0.0058 both ways), and the pinned bench
reads 164.28 -> 162.83 ms. The backward epilogue is covered only by fp64 kernel parity and a tiny
model. Does the speed base with F1 on train the same function at 5k?

## Hypothesis

F1 changes rounding only. The arm sits inside the seed spread of the G1 runs.

## Predictions

New arm: `nowta_pointer_fast_spdcells_speed_bf16c_cellp3` + `model.gemm_epilogue_fusion=true`
(K1 off), seeds 1 and 2. Controls: the filed S5 + G1 runs, CE@6 3.9023 / 3.9033, K1-K6
+0.0057 / +0.0067 (means 3.9028 / +0.0062; `successes/2026-10-13-g1-triton-gemm-5k.md`).

|---|---|---|---|

- Decision: F1 goes ON in `nowta_pointer_fast_spdcells_speed_bf16c` if the arm's mean CE@6 minus
  3.9028 is <= +0.003 AND its mean K1-K6 is >= +0.00589 (95 % of +0.0062). If CE@6 holds and

  1000 to 5000, and no step after 60 with gain above 0.95.

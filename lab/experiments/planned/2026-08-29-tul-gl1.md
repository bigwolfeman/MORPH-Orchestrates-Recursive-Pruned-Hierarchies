## Question

Does the unexplored cell — gisting mask (`tg_restrict`) + boundary-tap write with
FULL gradient + SIGReg on the written slot states + NO loop anywhere — produce the
first slot channel whose CONTENT is load-bearing (shuffle-sensitive), at an
acceptable CE cost vs its own unrestricted twin?

## Hypothesis

TG4b showed the pipe reads values (wrong-plan cost 0.48–0.56 nats) while shuffle
cost ~0 — the writes were homogeneous, not the reads absent. SIGReg at the write
attacks exactly that; the tap (not bag-mean) removes the measured −0.5 power-law
dilution; removing the loop removes both the takeover and the identity-map bias.
I put the headline gate at even odds — the homogeneity fix is proven machinery
applied at an unproven site.

## Predictions (frozen 2026-08-29, before the build completed)

- **L1 stability.** Both arms complete 4500 steps; no sustained detonation
  (`val/ce_tokens` >0.20 above running min for 2+ consecutive evals after 1000).
  New dynamics (gradient flows through slots into earlier spans) but no iterated
  map. Predict HOLDS.
- **L2 THE GATE — shuffle worth.** `worth_shuffle` ≥ 0.05 nats sustained (≥3
  consecutive evals) by 4500 on the restricted arm. Every prefix-interface arm:
  ≤ 0.0006. TG2's zero-worth was 0.03–0.155 but shuffle ~0. Predict ~50%.
- **L3 homogeneity dial.** SIGReg moves the writes: slot-state pairwise cos
  < 0.2 and eff_rank ≥ 64/1024 by 4500. Predict HOLDS (the FM targets moved in
  250 steps under the same regularizer).
- **L4 value sensitivity.** Wrong-plan probe cost ≥ 0.10 nats (TG family:
  0.48–0.56). Predict HOLDS — the mask makes it nearly structural.
- **L5 THE PRICE — restriction cost.** Restricted GL1 vs its unrestricted twin
  at matched steps: ≤ 0.15 nats on `val/ce_tokens` @4250. TG measured 0.17–0.42
  with the worse write; the tap+SIGReg+no-loop bet is that most of that gap was
  write quality. Predict: cost lands in [0.05, 0.20] — genuinely uncertain
  whether it clears the 0.15 line.
- **L6 instrument check.** Under the mask a span's first token cannot attend the
  previous span's tokens, so `first_tok_counterfactual` should flip positive
  near-structurally. If it stays negative, the metric or the mask is broken —
  investigate before interpreting anything else.

## Verdict rule

- SUCCESS = L1 & L2 & L5. → GL2 (the detached refiner loop on top of working
  writes), then GL3 (plan-as-router).
- L2 fires but L5 fails (> 0.15 nats): content-bearing slots exist but reliance
  is overpriced — GL3's router value and the CW/distill hybrids become the
  question; the result is still the program's first load-bearing content.
- L2 silent with L3 held (diverse writes, still shuffle-dead): the tap's content
  is not span-distinctive even when decorrelated — the write REPRESENTATION is
  the next suspect (what to write, not how); the trained-oracle ceiling run
  becomes mandatory before any further arm.
- L1 failing is new information about mask+gradient dynamics; its own finding.

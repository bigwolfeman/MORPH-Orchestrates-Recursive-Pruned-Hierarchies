## Question

Does MORPH's core loop earn depth when every position PAYS for it, with TUL
slots present in the sequence? The ladder established that the loop is inert
on the free axis (R0 K1−K6 = 0.0113, W1 0.0007, W2 0.0019 at step 5000)
regardless of seed rank. notul (paid, no slots) earns 0.2072 at 20k. A2 is
the 2x2's missing cell: paid + slots.

## Hypothesis

The loop's earning follows payment, not seeding. If tokens run the core, the
loop earns notul-like depth even with slot positions interleaved — the slots
were never the obstacle; the free ride was. Counter-hypothesis: TUL's layout
(token-state dropout, W_prefix scatter, slot positions in the core's
attention) interferes with the token-axis loop even when paid.

## Verdict

**SUCCESS — the hypothesis held: earning follows payment.** The loop earns
0.1685 nats of depth with tokens paying and slots present, vs <= 0.011 on
every free-ride arm regardless of seed. The TUL deficit was never the slots
and never the seed rank; it was that tokens skipped the core. A2 also posts
the best absolute CE of all five arms at 5k. Two calibration misses recorded
honestly: survival (P-A1) was overpriced at 60% given the guardless recipe's
instability, and P-A4's band was mis-specified (priced a cost, got a gain).

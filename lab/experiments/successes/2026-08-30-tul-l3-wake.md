# Planned: L3-WAKE — DB init, then standard AdamW full-BPTT to wake the loop

## Question

tul-l3 (DB-shaped: detached carry, per-iteration local mux) reached the ladder's
2nd-best CE (4.3519 @4250) with a loop that is CE-INERT: forced depth 1 vs 6
moves token CE 3.8683 → 3.8684 (paired rows, core_depth_sweep smoke). Does a
phase of standard end-to-end training (full BPTT, AdamW, fresh optimizer, LR
schedule restarted) on top of the DB weights make the iterations earn CE?

## Reference numbers (fixed)

l3 final 4.4005 / @4250 4.3519, depth-inert (Δ ≤ 0.001 nats, 3-row smoke —
the 48-row sweep queued before this run supersedes it as the pre-wake curve).

worth flat ~0.027. Coreless nomask 4.3102. Same-config CE replicate spread

## Results (filed 2026-08-30; artifacts in `../results/2026-08-30-tul-l3-wake/`)

Run tul-l3wake (wandb 026e4e9o), exit 0, init_from verified (495/495 tensors,
step-0 train loss 9.39 vs a fresh model's 22.5).

- **PW1 (85%): HELD.** S1 clean, no NaN. σ_max climbed to 4.10 though — warm-started

- **PW2 (35%, binding): the falsifier FIRED, as my prior leaned.** Post-wake depth
  sweep is dead flat: 4.2323 @1 vs 4.2327 @6 (|Δ|=0.0004 < 0.01). "DB init +

- **PW4 (30%): FAILED, as my prior leaned.** worth ≥0.04 streak of 2 at the end
  (0.042, 0.046) — misses the 3-consecutive bar, though rising at budget end.
- Generation: mediocre despite the CE record — topk50 rep4 0.0997/distinct3 0.828,
  greedy fully degenerate (0.893/0.092). CE and generation health dissociate again;

## Verdict

Success (all four predictions landed on the side my priors leaned). The capped twin
(2026-08-30-tul-l3-wake-cap.md, run the same night) is ALSO depth-flat, which resolves
the pair's three-way decision rule to: **DB init is an inert basin for depth. From-
scratch l2cap stays the loop recipe; DB init is dropped from the loop program** —
though DB-init + continued training stands as the best pure-CE recipe measured
(4.2044) and is worth a budget-matched test against 9000-step from-scratch l2cap.

## Updated hypothesis

The loop's depth-earning structure must form DURING training under contractivity
control; it cannot be retrofitted by gradient descent onto a converged one-pass
solution — the one-pass basin is too deep. Budget-matched follow-up (l2cap at 9000
steps vs DB→wake at 4500+4500) decides whether the wake CE record is the init or
just the steps.

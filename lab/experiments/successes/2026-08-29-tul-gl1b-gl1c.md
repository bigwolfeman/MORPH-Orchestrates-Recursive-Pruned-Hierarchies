## Question

Two independent levers on "the slots don't carry enough":
- **GL1b** — the MUX write-target loss (verified read, arXiv 2607.18264):
  span-multiplexed vocab-simplex targets, KL through the tied head, β=1.0,
  ρ=0.9, τ=1.0 (paper's Table 9), `mux_target: own`, SIGReg off. Does dense,
  provably-span-distinctive write supervision close part of the gap?
- **GL1c** — the gisting curriculum: the restricted model warm-started from the
  unrestricted twin's step_2000 (292/292 matched, 0 missing, 60 dropped, fresh
  optimizer — proven at real shapes). Does learning language before the mask
  close part of the gap?

Ruler for both: `gl1-ctrl-s1` val/ce_tokens @4250 = **4.6656** and GL1 restricted
@4250 = **5.0033**. GL1b's ruler is acknowledged not-mux-matched (a mux'd
unrestricted twin is a follow-up, not this experiment). GL1c carries a +2000-step
total-compute asymmetry vs GL1 — priced in the writeup, matched restricted-steps
is the comparison.

## Known risk, pre-committed handling (GL1b)

`mux_embed_grad_share = 0.399 at init` (builder-measured): the auxiliary owns
~40% of the tied embedding gradient, and detaching the head does NOT isolate it
(the path through the slot seed remains). This is FM2's interference channel.
Pre-registered fallback (the P1b pattern): if GL1b detonates (sustained rise
>0.20 for 2+ consecutive evals) OR ce_tokens @1500 exceeds GL1's 5.21 by more
than 0.30, ONE retry at `tul.mux_beta=0.25` is authorized, relabeled gl1b-lo,
predictions unchanged. No other dial may move.

## Verdict rule

- Either gate (M3/C3) passing at its arm's stability = that lever is REAL; the
  levers compose (gl1bc arm) next, plus GL2's refiner on the winner.
- Both gates fail with healthy runs = the price is not write-supervision-bound
  and not curriculum-bound at this budget → the 12k-step budget question and the
  faithful mid-prelude tap move to the front.
- M1 fallback firing is itself a finding (β=1.0 does not transfer to
  from-scratch at 209M) and does not disqualify M3 on the retry.

## Method

Smoke first (T5, after this freeze): GL1b ~150 steps + 1 eval (memory, mux_rel
sane, lift logs, no NaN); GL1c ~50 steps (banner + stability). Smoke wandb runs
named *-smoke and excluded from scoring. Real runs: sequential (UPS rule),
seed 1, panel keys + `training.ademamix_t_beta3=3500` + `tul.eval_ablations=true`,
wandb `gl1b-s1` / `gl1c-s1`, runner staged in morph-scratch/tulfm/. Checkpoints
pruned to finals after verdict.
